Android RSS reader

Your news, on your phone, and nowhere else.

Flash gathers the feeds you choose into one fast, quiet list. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and nothing that leaves your device unless you ask it to.

In closed testing

Flash is not yet on Google Play. It is currently being tested before release. If you have questions in the meantime, get in touch.

What it does

Keyword blocking that works

Add a word or a phrase and articles matching it disappear from every view, including ones already fetched. Matching runs on your device the moment a feed is parsed. No round trip, no waiting, no list of your interests sitting on a server somewhere.

Keyword alerts

The reverse of blocking. Name a subject you don't want to miss and Flash notifies you when it appears, then keeps a permanent record of every match in its own tab, grouped by term.

A reader that blocks ads and trackers

Articles open inside Flash, with a bundled blocklist stopping ad and tracker domains from loading and site notification prompts suppressed before they can appear. When a page can be reduced to plain text, Flash offers you that version instead.

AI summaries, on your device where possible

Four bullet points, in your language, at the length you choose. On phones with Gemini Nano the whole thing happens on the handset, offline and free. Only devices without it reach out to the cloud, and only when you tap the button.

Built for one hand

Folder tabs, navigation and every action sit in the bottom third of the screen, where your thumb already is. Material You throughout, five colour palettes, and a newspaper mode when you want something calmer.

Everything else

Folders, bookmarks, full-text search, an optional starter pack of popular feeds, background refresh on your schedule, a home screen unread widget, and a reading list that stays exactly where you left it. Available in English, German, Spanish, French and Italian.

What it doesn't do

  • No account, no sign-in, no password
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • No profile of you, no reading history collected
  • No location, camera, contacts or file access requested
  • No selling of anything about you, in either version, with ads or paid

Your feeds, folders, articles, read state, bookmarks and keyword lists are stored in a database on your phone. We hold no copy of any of it. The privacy policy lists every connection the app makes and why.

What it will cost

Flash is not on Google Play yet, so nothing below is live. Setting it out here so nobody is surprised later.

With ads

The whole app. Every feed, folder, filter, alert and bookmark. Ads in your feed, and a limit of a few AI summaries a day.

Paid, €5.99, once

No ads, no summary limit. One payment, not a subscription, and not a monthly fee for something that runs on your own phone.

The honest reason for the split: everything in Flash runs on your device and costs us nothing, except AI summaries on phones without Google's on-device model. Those go to a cloud model that charges per use. The ads pay for the summaries in the version with ads, and the purchase covers its own. Nothing else needed a price on it.

Flash comes in two versions: with ads and a daily limit on AI summaries, or a one-time purchase that removes both. There is no version without one or the other.

Who makes it

Flash is built by DVM Software, a one-person studio based in Barcelona, Spain. It exists because Palabre, a reader a lot of people relied on, was discontinued, and because its keyword blocking never quite worked. This one does.

Questions, bugs and feature requests all go to the same place: flashrssapp@gmail.com. A real person reads them.

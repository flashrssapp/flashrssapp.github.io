Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026. Applies to the Flash RSS Reader Android application, package io.getflash.app.

The short version. Flash has no accounts and no servers. We collect no personal data, Flash contains no analytics software, and everything you do inside the app is stored on your own device. The version with ads uses Google AdMob, described below. The sections below list every network connection the app can make and exactly what each one sends.

Who we are

Flash RSS Reader is published by DVM Software, a sole trader based in Barcelona, Spain, and the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

You can reach us at any time at flashrssapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server, no database and no account system. We cannot see your feeds, what you read, what you block, what you bookmark or when you open the app, because none of that is ever sent to us.

All of the following is written to a database stored privately on your device and stays there:

  • The feeds you subscribe to and the folders you organise them into
  • Articles fetched from those feeds, and which of them you have read
  • Your bookmarks
  • Your keyword blocklist and keyword alerts, and the alerts they have caught
  • Your settings

Permissions the app requests

Internet access
To fetch your feeds and open articles. Without it the app cannot do anything.
Notifications
Two separate switches in Settings, both off until you turn them on: Unread articles (a notification and the app-icon badge with how many new articles arrived) and Keyword alerts (a notification when a keyword you set up matches an article).
Advertising ID (version with ads only)
Read by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads, within the choices you make in the consent form. The paid version never starts the ads software.
Run at startup, and prevent sleeping
So scheduled background refreshes survive a reboot and can finish once started.

Flash does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, phone or file storage access.

About the AI summary

Summarising is the only feature that can send the content of an article anywhere, and it does so only when you press the button.

On phones with Google's on-device model (Gemini Nano), the summary is made on your phone and nothing leaves it.

Otherwise, or if an article is too long for the on-device model, the article's cleaned text is sent to Google's Gemini service, through Firebase AI Logic, to make the summary. On the free Gemini tier, Google may use that text to improve its services. Flash never stores, reads or shares the article or the summary on any server, and nobody at Flash sees it. The app says this in a short sheet before the first summary is sent. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.

About backups

Flash relies on Android Auto Backup, a feature of the Android operating system, which copies app data to your own Google Drive account and restores it when you reinstall or move to a new phone. This is done by Android under your own device settings, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.

Separately, Flash can export your feeds, folders and blocklist to a file that you save wherever you choose. That file goes only where you send it.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Flash contains no analytics library and no crash-reporting library. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share information about you. The only advertising is in the version with ads, through Google AdMob, as described below.

The in-app reader actively blocks known advertising and tracking domains while you read, and suppresses websites' notification permission prompts before they can be shown. That stays true in every version.

Advertising in the version with ads

Flash comes in two versions only: one with ads in your feed and a daily limit on AI summaries, and a one-time paid unlock with no advertising. There is no version without one or the other.

  • The advertising provider is Google AdMob. It receives your advertising ID, device and usage data (such as your device model, IP address and how you interact with ads) for ads and measurement. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choice, as EU law requires. If you do not consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, under Privacy options.
  • Ads appear only in the main feed. Never in the reader, your bookmarks, keywords, search or the summary sheet.
  • The paid version shows no ads and never starts the ads software.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment. Flash only learns whether the unlock was bought; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Flash is a general-audience news reader. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over personal data a company holds about you. In our case there is nothing to exercise them against, because we hold no data about you.

The data inside the app belongs to you and is under your control: you can delete any feed, article, bookmark or keyword at any time, export your subscriptions to a file, and remove everything permanently by uninstalling the app.

If you believe your data has been mishandled you may complain to your national supervisory authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of the page changes with it, and any change that affects what the app sends will be described in the release notes for the version that introduces it.
